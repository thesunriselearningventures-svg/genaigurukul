FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need any prior knowledge to join?+
No prior knowledge is required for most courses. Our Excel, SQL, and Python courses start from absolute basics. For the Generative AI track, a basic understanding of Python is helpful but not mandatory — we'll cover what you need.
Are the sessions recorded or live?+
All sessions are live, offline, and in-person at our Kandivali East classroom. This is by design — the real classroom environment leads to better learning, faster doubt resolution, and stronger accountability.
What are the batch timings?+
We offer both weekday batches (morning and evening) and weekend batches to accommodate students and working professionals. Contact us to know the current schedule and available slots.
Will I get a certificate?+
Yes. You receive a GenAI Gurukul certificate of completion for every course or track you finish. We also help you build a project portfolio that is far more valuable to employers than any certificate.
How is this better than an online course?+
Online courses have completion rates below 5% because there's no accountability, no real doubt-solving, and no personalisation. At GenAI Gurukul you attend a physical classroom, work on real projects with a trainer who knows your name, and get your doubts resolved immediately. See our full comparison on the Why Us page.
Do you help with placements?+
We provide career assistance including resume building, interview preparation, and career guidance. While we don't guarantee placements, our focus on real projects and practical skills significantly improves your chances in the job market.
